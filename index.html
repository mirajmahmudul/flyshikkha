Questions

Do I need to speak Korean?

Many degree programs are taught in English. Some ask for an English test such as IELTS, others ask for Korean (TOPIK). Your counselor checks the rule for each program on your list.

Which documents will I upload?

Passport, academic transcripts, an English test result, and a statement of purpose. Your checklist shows what is approved and what is still missing.

Does booking a call cost anything?

No. You can book a call on this site without making any payment.
